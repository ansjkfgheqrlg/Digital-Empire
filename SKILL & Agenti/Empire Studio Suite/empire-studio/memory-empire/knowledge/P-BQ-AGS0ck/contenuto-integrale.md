## PARTE 3 — PASSO 1: CRAFT THE HOOK (integrale)

**Definizione** (3:21–3:29): *"The hook is how you capture the person's attention and leave
them hanging."*

**Storia esempio, integrale** (3:29–5:29): *"My story started with this simple hook. I was
about to get on stage in Calgary in 2009 with the Dalai Lama and I was so unbelievably
nervous. While about to get on stage with this holy man, this world visionary, I decided to pop
into the hotel bar in the middle of the day and asked for two shots of vodka."* Seguito da:
*"the story goes on to explain and describe in vivid detail me by the bar sweating, nervous,
pounding back shots of vodka before going on the stage with a global religious leader."*

**Perché funziona — "idea collision"**: *"great hooks collide facts... It's called idea
collision. You take two fundamentally different ideas and you smash them together. What were
the two fundamentally different ideas here? Religious leader and shots of vodka. The human mind
is not used to hearing both these ideas in the same sentence. This makes it a hook."*

**Versione social accorciata (hook nei primi 3 secondi)**, mostrata a video (frame-081): *"I
can't believe the Dalai Lama made me kick back shots of vodka."* seguita da: *"So what happened
was I was invited to share the stage with the Dalai Lama when he was visiting Calgary. But I
was so absolutely nervous. I ran into the hotel bar and I started kicking back shots of vodka.
This is what happened next."*

**Citazione di Jay Shetty** (foto reale in bianco e nero, frame-091): *"The first 3 seconds,
hook them. And then all the way up to 11th second, go deeper in the hook. That's when you start
the story." — Jay Shetty.*

**Estensione ad altri formati** (frame-086): *"You Can Also Apply These To • Stage Talk •
Presentation"* — con più tempo a disposizione dal vivo rispetto ai social.

**Secondo esempio — Naveen Jain**: *"Just 2 days ago, I was on stage at a conference here in
London and I was asked to interview the billionaire Naveen Jain."* Citazione mostrata a video
(frame-106): *"I make my kids see me working." — Naveen Jain.* Contesto completo: *"I don't buy
that I need to spend all my free time with my kids... When my kids wake up on Sunday morning
and they come to the living room, I'm not sitting in front of the TV. I'm not reading a book.
I'm on my laptop working. They see me working late at night. They see how passionate I am for
my job."*

Analisi dell'idea collision di Naveen: *"as parents, and I'm a parent, we are told when your
kids are around you, don't worry. Be attentive to them. Focus on them. Naveen, whether you agree
or not, he created an idea collision."*

**Correzione via lower-third reale (frame-101)**: il figlio di Naveen citato come "uno dei più
giovani miliardari" è **Ankur Jain, Founder and CEO of Bilt Rewards** — corregge la
trascrizione automatica ("his son is Uncle Jane").

**Nota su b-roll etichettato**: frame-111 mostra una scena (padre al laptop, bambini di
spalle) con etichetta esplicita **"Representational"** in basso a destra — dichiarata come
