# Retention Hooks — 12 Anti-Scroll Techniques

Retention hooks are phrases inserted in the MIDDLE of the video to prevent
scrolling during the payload. Essential for videos >30 seconds.

## Placement Rule

One retention hook every 20-30 seconds in the payload.

| Video Duration | Hooks Needed | Positions |
|---------------|-------------|-----------|
| ≤20 sec | 0 | — |
| 30 sec | 1 | ~15 sec |
| 45 sec | 1 | ~22 sec |
| 60 sec | 2 | ~20 sec, ~40 sec |
| 90 sec | 3 | ~25 sec, ~50 sec, ~70 sec |
| 120 sec | 4 | ~25 sec, ~50 sec, ~75 sec, ~100 sec |
| 180 sec | 5 | ~30 sec, ~55 sec, ~80 sec, ~110 sec, ~140 sec |

Trend videos: NO retention hooks needed. The format itself maintains attention.

---

## Category 1: Curiosity

**RH-01:** "Ma aspetta, perché il terzo punto è il più importante."
→ Creates anticipation for the next point. Place between framework points.

**RH-02:** "E qui arriva la parte che nessuno ti dice..."
→ "Nobody tells you" = exclusive information trigger. Place before the main insight.

**RH-03:** "Ora ti mostro la parte interessante."
→ Explicit promise of imminent value. Place before showing data/screenshots/results.

## Category 2: Pattern Interrupt

**RH-04:** "Stop. Prima che vai avanti, guarda questo."
→ "Stop" interrupts mental flow. Works as an alarm signal.
   **Always accompany with a visual change** (framing change or show screenshot).

**RH-05:** "Ok, dimenticati tutto quello che ho detto finora. QUESTO è il punto vero."
→ Mental reset. Very powerful. **Use MAX 1 time per video.**

## Category 3: Social Proof Mid-Video

**RH-06:** "Con un cliente, questo singolo cambiamento ha generato [risultato]."
→ Social proof inserted mid-video. Adds credibility to the current point.
   Place after explaining a theoretical concept, before the practical example.

**RH-07:** "E non lo dico io — lo dicono i dati."
→ Transfers authority from person to data. More credible than "trust me."
   Place after a strong or counterintuitive statement. Show data immediately after.

## Category 4: Rhetorical Question

**RH-08:** "Vuoi sapere perché la maggior parte non lo fa?"
→ Creates curiosity about the "why." Place after presenting a solution,
   before explaining why it's neglected.

**RH-09:** "Indovina cosa è successo dopo?"
→ Classic cliffhanger. Creates dramatic pause before the result.
   Place after "what we did" and before "result" in Proof videos.

## Category 5: Direct Challenge

**RH-10:** "Scommetto che il tuo funnel ha questo problema adesso."
→ Personal challenge. The viewer thinks "is that right?" and stays to verify.
   Place after showing a common error or data about a widespread problem.

**RH-11:** "Fermati e vai a controllare il tuo sito. Ci vediamo tra 10 secondi."
→ Invitation to immediate mid-video action. Even if they don't do it,
   the fact that you MIGHT be right keeps them glued.
   Place after showing a very specific, easily verifiable error.

## Category 6: Internal Cliffhanger

**RH-12:** "Ma il vero motivo per cui funziona... non è quello che pensi."
→ Cliffhanger promising a twist. The viewer has already invested time
   and wants to know the "real reason."
   Place before the "why it worked" section (Proof) or before the takeaway.

---

## Selection by Video Type

| Video Type | Best Retention Hooks |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Educational | RH-01, RH-02, RH-06, RH-12 (curiosity + social proof) |
| Proof | RH-09, RH-06, RH-12 (rhetorical + social proof + cliffhanger) |
| Action (>30s) | RH-04, RH-10 (pattern interrupt + challenge) |
| Trend | None needed |

**Rule of thumb:** Vary categories within the same video. Don't use two curiosity
hooks back-to-back. Mix: curiosity → social proof → challenge.
