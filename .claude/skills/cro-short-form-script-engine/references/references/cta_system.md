# CTA System — 6 Templates + Decision Matrix

## The 6 CTA Templates

### CTA #1: Lead Magnet via Keyword Comment 💬
**Rank:** Primary — 3-4 videos out of 7 per week

**Script:**
"Ho una checklist con 25 punti per controllare il tuo funnel.
Commenta 'AUDIT' e te la mando."

**Variants:**
- "Vuoi i 25 punti da controllare sul tuo funnel? Commenta 'AUDIT' — te la mando in DM."
- "Ho creato una checklist per controllare tutto questo. Commenta 'AUDIT' e la ricevi gratis."
- "Commenta 'AUDIT' e ti mando la checklist con 25 punti per il tuo funnel. Gratis, zero fuffa."

**Suggested keywords:** AUDIT, CRO, CHECK, FUNNEL, FIX
**Best platform:** TikTok (engagement boost + ManyChat trigger)
**Use when:** Action videos with practical fix, Educational videos, any video mentioning a checklist point

**Mechanism:** The keyword comment triggers ManyChat (or DM automation) that sends
the lead magnet link automatically. Double benefit: lead capture + algorithm boost
(comment = engagement).

---

### CTA #2: Lead Magnet via Link in Bio 🔗
**Rank:** Secondary — 1-2 videos per week

**Script:** "Link in bio per la CRO Checklist gratuita."

**Variants:**
- "Se vuoi controllare il tuo funnel punto per punto, link in bio — checklist gratuita."
- "Link in bio → CRO Checklist con 25 punti. Gratis."

**Best platform:** Instagram Reel (clickable link in bio)
**Use when:** Short videos (<20 sec), Trend videos, IG Reel format

**Note:** Only works if the link in bio is updated with the lead magnet.
On TikTok: less effective than keyword comment (requires more steps).

---

### CTA #3: Follow ➕
**Rank:** Tertiary — 1 video per week max

**Script:** "Segui per altri teardown come questo."

**Variants:**
- "Se vuoi vedere altri errori CRO come questi, seguimi."
- "Ogni settimana analizzo funnel e checkout. Segui per non perderti i prossimi."

**Best platform:** TikTok and IG Reel (both)
**Use when:** Trend videos with high reach but cold target, viral content

**Warning:** NEVER use as primary CTA. It's the weakest CTA for lead generation.
Always specify WHAT they'll see ("teardowns", "CRO tips") not just "follow me for more content."

---

### CTA #4: Strategy Call 📞
**Rank:** Selective — 0-1 videos per week, warm audience only

**Script:**
"Se vuoi che analizzi il TUO funnel in 15 minuti,
link in bio per prenotare. È gratis."

**Variants:**
- "Vuoi sapere dove il tuo funnel perde conversioni? Link in bio — 15 minuti, gratis."
- "15 minuti. Ti mostro i 3 errori più gravi nel tuo funnel. Gratis. Link in bio per prenotare."

**Best platform:** Instagram Reel (warmer, more business-oriented audience)
**Use when:** After Proof/Case Study videos (viewer just saw results), only with warm audience

**Warning:** NEVER use with cold audience. NEVER more than 1/week.
Too many "book a call" videos = salesman, not expert.

---

### CTA #5: Save 🔖
**Rank:** Complementary — 1 video per week

**Script:** "Salva questo video. La prossima volta che apri il tuo funnel, ti servirà."

**Variants:**
- "Salvati questo video — ti servirà quando lavorerai sulla tua landing page."
- "Bookmark questo. Quando rifai il funnel, riapri e controlla punto per punto."

**Best platform:** Instagram Reel (saves weigh heavily in IG algorithm)
**Use when:** Educational videos with reapplicable framework/method

**Note:** Can be combined with lead magnet CTA in the CAPTION
(voice = save, caption = comment AUDIT).

---

### CTA #6: Engagement Comment 💬
**Rank:** Occasional — 1 video per week

**Script:**
"Fammi vedere il tuo checkout nei commenti
— ti dico il primo errore che trovo."

**Variants:**
- "Manda il link della tua landing page nei commenti — ti faccio un mini-audit in 30 secondi."
- "Quanti campi ha il tuo form di checkout? Scrivilo nei commenti — ti dico se sono troppi."

**Best platform:** TikTok (comments weigh heavily in TikTok algorithm)
**Use when:** Teardown videos, when you want to generate future content material

**Important:** RESPOND TO COMMENTS. If you ask for engagement and don't respond, you lose credibility.

## Decision Matrix

**By video type:**
| Video Type | Primary CTA | Secondary CTA |
|-----------|-------------|---------------|
| Action | #1 Keyword Comment | #5 Save |
| Educational | #1 Keyword Comment | #5 Save |
| Proof | #4 Strategy Call | #1 Keyword Comment |
| Trend | #3 Follow | #2 Link in Bio |

**By platform:**
| Platform | Preferred CTA | Reason |
|----------|--------------|--------|
| TikTok | #1 Keyword Comment | Comments = engagement + ManyChat trigger |
| IG Reel | #2 Link in Bio | Clickable link + saves matter |
| Stories | #2 Link in Bio | Direct link sticker to lead magnet |

---

## CTA Rules (Non-Negotiable)

**Mandatory:**
- ONE CTA per video. Never two.
- Repeat CTA in the caption
- Be specific about what they GET ("checklist with 25 points" > "a free resource")
- Keyword comment: single word, easy to type, ALL CAPS (AUDIT, CRO, CHECK)

**Forbidden:**
- "Follow me for more content" as primary CTA (weakest CTA possible)
- Ambiguous CTA ("discover more", "click here")
- Double CTA ("comment AND follow AND link in bio")
- CTA disconnected from video content
