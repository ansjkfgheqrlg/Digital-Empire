# Outline — The Coven of Lost Ember

## Premessa
Four women who have every reason to distrust one another are the only ones left standing over a crack in the world. A fugitive running from her own coven, a widow who swore off magic the day it killed her husband, a seventeen year old who has never once controlled what lives in her hands, and the warden who has spent two years hunting the first of them. The seal under the village well is failing, it widens every night it goes unmended, and the binding that could close it needs four witches joined in trust. None of them has any intention of giving it.

## Ambientazione
**Lost Ember**, a village of four hundred people in a steep northern valley, present day. Eighty years ago it had a coven of its own. It does not any more, and nobody under sixty remembers why that used to matter. Beneath the village green sits **Ember Well**, capped in 1911 with a stone lid nobody has lifted since, and under the well is the thing the old coven bound there and passed down the duty of holding.

## Personaggi
- **Wren Halloway** (28, the fugitive): vuole To stay ahead of the Ashmoor coven long enough to disappear for good. Segreto: she did not leave Ashmoor empty handed. She took their hearthstone, and she has been carrying it in a wool sock at the bottom of her bag for eleven weeks, and it is the reason the ground under Lost Ember started to move the week she arrived.
- **Marged Pryce** (61, the widow): vuole To be left alone, and to never again be responsible for what magic does to the people standing next to her. Segreto: she has not lost the craft. She can still feel a working assemble itself behind her ribs the moment she stops guarding against it, and what frightens her is not that it is gone but how badly she still wants it.
- **Nessa Quill** (17, the orphan): vuole To find one single person who is not afraid of her. Segreto: she felt the crack open four days before it opened, in her teeth and her sleep, and she has never told anyone that things underground have been speaking to her since she was nine.
- **Bel Carrow** (45, the enemy): vuole To bring Wren Halloway in, close the file, and be reassigned somewhere with fewer hills. Segreto: she requested this posting. Her own sister vanished in a village exactly like this one, in a collapse exactly like this one, and the Assize told her it had been an accident.
- **Owen Pryce** (Marged's late husband, eleven years dead): vuole nothing now. Segreto: the working that killed him was not his. Marged laid the last line of it, and no one alive knows that except her.
- **Ada Fenwick** (79, keeper of the parish records): vuole To hand the village's history to someone before she dies. Segreto: she has known since 1974 that the well needed tending and has watched three generations decide it was somebody else's job.
- **Ruth Sedgewick** (Ashmoor's first, Wren's former teacher): vuole To recover the hearthstone and make an example of the girl who took it. Segreto: she let Wren take it. Ashmoor needed the stone to travel to a village with a failing seal, and Wren was the courier who did not know she was one.
- **Gil Tanner** (34, runs the village garage): vuole To keep his daughter safe and get the road reopened. Segreto: he saw the green move on the second night and told nobody, because saying it out loud would make it true.
- **Assize Warden Hollis** (Bel's superior): vuole To keep the incident classified and the village quiet. Segreto: the Assize has known for forty years that villages without covens fail, and has been letting them, because what comes up through a broken seal is worth more to them than four hundred people.
- **Nessa's mother** (never named, absent): vuole nothing that anyone knows. Segreto: she left Nessa at Lost Ember's church door in 2009 and not at any of the four towns closer to where she lived, and that was a choice, not an accident.

## Atti
1. A crack opens across the village green on a Tuesday night. Four women who want nothing to do with each other are the only ones in Lost Ember who can see it for what it is, and Ada Fenwick's parish books tell them the only thing that will close it is a binding worked by four joined witches. Their first attempt fails badly enough to put a man in hospital.
2. Working in a hostility that never quite lets up, they learn the binding does not fail for lack of skill. It fails because the joining is real, and each of them is holding something back that the working can feel. The crack widens each night. At its worst point Bel finds the hearthstone in Wren's bag, the group breaks apart in the middle of the green with the ground open behind them, and Wren runs.
3. Ada's oldest book gives them the line the newer copies had softened: the binding does not ask for trust, it asks to be told the truth. One at a time, in the worst week of their lives, the four of them say the thing they have never said. They close the well, they learn what the Assize has been doing to villages like this one for forty years, and Lost Ember gets a coven again, which is not the same thing as getting an ending.

## Capitoli
01. **The Tuesday It Started** — Nessa Quill wakes at 3 a.m. with her jaw aching and the taste of pennies, and by morning there is a hairline crack running the length of the village green that the council photographs and blames on the drought. Wren Halloway, eleven days into a room above the pub she paid for in cash, feels it through the floorboards and starts packing.
02. **A Woman Who Does Not Do That Any More** — Marged Pryce is asked by the parish council to look at the crack, because everyone in Lost Ember over sixty still quietly thinks of her as the person you ask, and she refuses on her doorstep without letting them finish the sentence. That night she cannot sleep for the pull of it, a working assembling itself behind her ribs uninvited for the first time in eleven years.
03. **The Warden** — Bel Carrow arrives in Lost Ember with a file, a rented car and two years of tracking behind her, and finds the woman she came for standing in the middle of the green at dusk looking down into a crack that was six inches wide that morning and is a foot wide now. Neither of them runs, because both of them have just understood the other can see it.
04. **What Ada Knows** — Ada Fenwick, who has kept the parish records for fifty one years, opens her back room to the three of them and produces a ledger of well tendings that stops abruptly in 1944. The village had a coven. It stopped having one. Nobody has lifted the well lid in a hundred and fourteen years.
05. **The Girl on the Green** — Nessa is found at midnight sitting at the very edge of the crack with her bare hands flat on the grass, and when Wren pulls her back the ground closes two inches on its own. All three older women understand at once what she is, and Nessa understands, for the first time in her life, that she is not broken.
06. **Four Is the Number** — The oldest tending record specifies a binding worked by four joined, and there are exactly four women in Lost Ember who can feel the thing under the well. Marged says no. Bel says she is here to arrest one of them, not join her. Wren says she is leaving Thursday. They agree to try it once.
07. **The First Attempt** — On the green at 2 a.m. they lay the circle from Ada's diagram, and the working takes hold for nine seconds and then turns. The backlash throws Gil Tanner, watching from his gate, into a wall and puts him in hospital in Kendal with a fractured skull, and the crack is wider at dawn than it was at midnight.
08. **The Blame** — In the aftermath the four of them tear into each other in Ada's kitchen, each certain the failure came from one of the others, and Bel formally arrests Wren, and Marged tells all of them she is finished. Fine Atto I: they scatter, and that night the crack crosses the road and the first stone comes out of the church wall.
09. **Nobody Is Coming** — Bel calls in the incident and is told, politely and finally, that the Assize is not sending anyone and that she is to secure the site and await reassignment. She sits in her car for an hour, then goes and unlocks the room she has been holding Wren in.
10. **The Widow's Hands** — Marged goes back to the well alone at first light to prove to herself she can look at it without wanting it, and is still there at noon. Ada finds her, and tells her about Owen Pryce's funeral and what the village decided about her that day without ever asking her a single question.
11. **What Nessa Hears** — Nessa takes Wren down to the culvert where the beck runs under the green and shows her that the thing below is not raging, it is speaking, and has been speaking to her since she was nine years old. Wren, who has spent eleven years being told what she is by people who never asked, is the first person to simply listen.
12. **The Second Attempt** — Better prepared, better placed, all four sober and cooperating. It holds for four minutes, long enough for hope, and then unravels along exactly the same seam. Nobody is hurt this time, and that is worse, because it means the failure is not an accident of circumstance.
13. **The Seam** — Ada's diagram has a marginal note in a nineteenth century hand: *it will not knit where a thread is held back.* They realise the working is not failing because they lack skill or practice. It is failing because it can feel that all four of them are lying about something.
14. **Bel's File** — Cornered by the others into going first, Bel opens her own file on the table and admits she requested this posting, that her sister Iris vanished in a village called Hartsett in 2011 in a collapse the Assize called an accident, and that she has been looking for a second one ever since. The others see her, for the first time, as a person with a wound rather than a badge.
15. **The Sock at the Bottom of the Bag** — Bel, being who she is, searches Wren's room anyway, out of habit and two years of instinct, and finds the Ashmoor hearthstone. She confronts her with it on the green in front of the other two, and the ground answers the stone audibly, and every one of them hears it.
16. **What Wren Took** — Wren admits she took it and that she has known since the second night that the crack follows her. She does not admit the rest, because she does not yet know the rest. Marged calls her a danger to four hundred people, Nessa begs her to stay, and Wren walks out of Lost Ember at two in the morning with the stone in her coat. Fine Atto II.
17. **Twelve Miles of Road** — Wren gets twelve miles before the stone goes cold in her hand, and understands, standing at a bus shelter in the rain at dawn, that Ruth Sedgewick did not fail to stop her leaving Ashmoor with it. She was allowed. She was sent. She turns around and walks back.
18. **The Older Book** — Ada, who has been quietly dying for four months and has told nobody, brings out the 1839 volume the later copies were transcribed from, and the line everyone has been reading as *worked by four joined in trust* is in the older hand *worked by four who have told each other true.* Trust was a Victorian softening. The requirement was always confession.
19. **Marged Says It** — Marged tells the other three what she has not said aloud in eleven years: the working that killed Owen was hers. He was holding it open and she laid the last line wrong, and she has let an entire village believe it was his mistake because they offered her that story at the funeral and she took it.
20. **Nessa Says It** — Nessa admits she felt the crack four days early and said nothing because for four days she was somebody who mattered, and that she has been talking to the thing under the well since she was nine, and that some nights she has wanted it to come up.
21. **Ruth Sedgewick Comes to Lost Ember** — Ashmoor arrives to collect its property, and Ruth confirms with complete calm what Wren worked out at the bus shelter: the stone was meant to travel to a village with a failing seal, and Ashmoor has been doing this for the Assize for a long time. Bel arrests her own order's contractor with no authority whatsoever to do it.
22. **Wren Says It** — With the green splitting and the church wall coming down, Wren tells them the last of it: she did not run from Ashmoor because they were cruel. She ran because they asked her to do this in a village called Hartsett in 2011, and she did it, and she has known what she was carrying ever since. Bel, whose sister died at Hartsett, has to choose in the space of one breath.
23. **The Binding** — Four women who have told each other the truth work the binding at the mouth of Ember Well, with Ada holding the diagram and half the village holding lanterns, and it knits. What comes up before it closes is seen by four hundred people, which is the point, and is why the Assize will not be able to file this one as a drought.
24. **A Coven Again** — Six weeks later: Ada's funeral, and the parish records handed to Nessa in front of everyone. Gil Tanner walks with a stick and brings his daughter to meet them anyway. Bel resigns from the Assize and stays, which surprises nobody but Bel. On the first Sunday of the month, four women lift the lid of Ember Well and look in, the way somebody was supposed to have been doing all along.
