# Prompt copertina — The Coven of Lost Ember

Da dare a un generatore di immagini. Il titolo deve essere disegnato DENTRO l'immagine:
il codice non lo riscrive sopra.

---

## Prompt (inglese, da incollare intero)

Vertical 2:3 book cover, high resolution, cozy fantasy with an undercurrent of dread.

SCENE: four women standing in a loose, uneven ring on a village green at night, seen from
slightly above and behind, none of them touching. They are deliberately not a united group:
one stands a full pace outside the ring with her arms folded, one is very young and barefoot
in the grass, one is old and holds a lantern low at her hip, one has her coat collar up like
a woman about to leave. Between their feet, running the width of the ring, a jagged crack in
the turf glows a low ember orange from far below, as if the ground were lit from inside by a
banked fire. Thin threads of orange light rise out of the crack and hang in the cold air.

SETTING: an English northern village at night. Behind the women, a low stone church tower
and four or five slate roofed cottages with two windows lit. A capped stone well sits at the
edge of the green, its heavy round lid still in place, moss along the rim. Steep dark fells
rise behind the village and fill the upper background. Bare hedgerows, a dry stone wall, an
overturned bucket.

ATMOSPHERE AND LIGHT: two light sources only, in tension. Cold blue moonlight on the fells,
the roofs and the women's shoulders, and warm ember orange coming up out of the crack from
below, catching under their chins and along the hems of their coats. Mist to knee height
across the green. Autumn, damp, still. Mood: quiet, watchful, beautiful, wrong.

PALETTE: deep midnight blue, slate grey, wet black stone and bracken brown, against a narrow
band of ember orange and a little amber from the lit windows. No other saturated colour
anywhere. The orange must read as heat from underground, not as firelight.

STYLE: painterly digital illustration with the texture of oil on board, visible brushwork in
the sky and the mist, crisp edges on the stonework. Literary and restrained, in the tradition
of prestige folk horror and cozy fantasy hardback jackets. Fine film grain over the whole
image. No photorealism, no 3D render, no glossy CGI, no anime, no cartoon.

COMPOSITION: the village and the fells occupy the lower two thirds. The four figures stand
across the middle, small enough that no face is readable. The glowing crack leads the eye
from the bottom edge up into the ring. The top third is empty night sky and mist,
deliberately kept dark and low contrast so the title sits on it cleanly. A narrower clear
band across the very bottom for the author name.

TEXT ON THE COVER, rendered exactly and spelled letter for letter:

Title, across the empty upper third, in four stacked lines, centred:
THE
COVEN
OF LOST
EMBER

Set in a high contrast classical serif with sharp thin serifs, in the manner of Didot or
Bodoni, in warm bone white, with a very faint ember coloured glow behind the letterforms so
they stay legible against the dark sky. Generous letter spacing. The word COVEN is the widest
line and sets the measure. The title block takes at least a third of the image width and must
stay readable at thumbnail size.

Subtitle, immediately under the title, centred, much smaller, in spaced small capitals, in
muted ember orange:
A FOUND FAMILY WITCH COVEN NOVEL

Author name, along the bottom clear band, centred, small capitals, bone white, about half the
height of the subtitle:
MAREN ASHCROFT

NEGATIVE, do not include: any other text, no taglines, no review quotes, no publisher logo,
no watermark, no signature, no borders, no decorative frames, no ornamental corners, no faces
looking at the viewer, no pointed hats, no broomsticks, no cauldrons, no pentagrams, no
glowing eyes, no skulls, no blood, no cats, no modern cars, no people other than the four
women.
