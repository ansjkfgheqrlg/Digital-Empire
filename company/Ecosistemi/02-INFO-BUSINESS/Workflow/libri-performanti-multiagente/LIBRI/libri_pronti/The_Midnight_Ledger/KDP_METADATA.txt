Titolo: The Midnight Ledger
Autore: Maren Ashcroft
Sottotitolo: A Paranormal Bookshop Mystery
Nicchia/keyword: paranormal bookshop cozy mystery

Descrizione (back cover):
A dead man left her a bookshop. He also left her four hundred pencil notes, and the last thirty years of his life spent proving what happened to a girl in a churchyard.

Clara Vance has never heard of Edwin Pryor. She has certainly never heard of Fenn Abbas. So when a solicitor's letter hands her a crooked bookshop in a Dorset market town, along with eight thousand unsold books and a cup still standing on the counter, she comes with one plan: value it, sell it, go home.

Then, on her second night, she wakes at ten past three to the sound of somebody upstairs turning pages.

The room above the shop holds six hundred books that Edwin Pryor would not sell to anybody, for any money, for thirty one years. None of them is worth more than a few pounds. Every one of them has a line of pencil hidden in a margin, and not one of those lines has anything to do with the book it is written in.

*she did not have a coat with her*

*13 minutes is not enough time*

*because I told her he would come*

In the churchyard forty feet from the front door there is a stone: RUTH FELTHAM, 1974 to 1996. Twenty two years old, a shop assistant, found on the path one November morning. Accidental death. A file four pages long. And a whole town that will talk to Clara about anything except her.

THE MIDNIGHT LEDGER is a paranormal cozy mystery about a book restorer who inherits a puzzle instead of a business: a hidden index built out of shop ledgers and margins by a lonely man who could not go to the police and would not let it be forgotten. It is about a detective with eighteen months of funding and no new evidence, a witness who told the truth once in 1996 and was never written down, and a bookshop that will not stay quiet until somebody finally reads it properly.

Because Edwin Pryor did not leave Clara Vance a building.

He left her the one thing he could never say out loud.

Bio autore (campo 'Author Bio' su KDP):
Maren Ashcroft writes cozy fantasy and paranormal mystery about ordinary places with something old underneath them: village bookshops, moorland wells, the buildings nobody thinks to ask about. Her books are quiet, and then they are not.
