Titolo: The Second-Hand Spellbook
Autore: Maren Ashcroft
Sottotitolo: A Cozy Fantasy Novel

Descrizione (back cover):
Maren Vessel came back to Saltmarch to sell a bookshop. She was there four days before she found the ledger.

Her great-aunt Ilse kept records for sixty years: every box bought, every price paid, in the same small upright hand. But at the back of the seventh ledger there are nine pages ruled into four columns, headed with one word: LENT.

The first column is a date. The second is a name. The third is what that person got back, and it is never anything useful. A hymn tune. The smell of a shed. The way a man laughed. The last entry is from 2015 and then there are eleven years of nothing at all.

It is the fourth column Maren cannot work out.

On the high shelf, ten feet up, there are forty books with no titles on their spines and no words on any of their pages. Lend one to somebody and they get back a small thing they had lost, something too ordinary to have been worth keeping and too far gone to reach.

The price is real. The price is not paid by the person who asks.

THE SECOND-HAND SPELLBOOK is a cozy fantasy about a bookshop, a dying town, a council that wants the building, and a woman working out what her aunt spent fifty-four years paying for. There is tea, a grey cat, a man next door who comes through the wall with bread, and an argument at a planning meeting.

Nobody is in danger. Nothing chases anyone. It will keep you up anyway.
