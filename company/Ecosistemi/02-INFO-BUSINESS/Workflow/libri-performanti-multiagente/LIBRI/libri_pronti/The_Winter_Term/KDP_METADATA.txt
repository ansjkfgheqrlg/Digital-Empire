Titolo: The Winter Term
Autore: Maren Ashcroft
Sottotitolo: A Dark Academia Mystery
Nicchia/keyword: dark academia mystery

Descrizione (back cover):
Nell Kavanagh gets eleven days' notice.

A scholarship nobody advertised, at a boarding school on the Northumberland moors, starting in the middle of the winter term. Her mother says it out loud before Nell can: they don't do that. They don't just give it to you in December.

They put her in room fourteen, at the top of the West Tower, where the inside of the window freezes. The wardrobe is still full of another girl's clothes.

Her name was Cassia. Everyone says she withdrew on the fourteenth of January, for family reasons. Her withdrawal letter is in the office, typed and signed and dated. Her father, Nell will learn, died four years earlier.

And under the mattress there is a laundry docket, stamped by the school, for two shirts and a skirt collected from room fourteen on the fifteenth of January. The day after.

Cassia's five friends do not tell Nell to mind her own business. That is the first thing that frightens her. They bring her up to the warm room. They give her the good blanket. They answer every question she asks, one at a time, in the same words, and their five accounts agree on everything except who rang the chapel bell that night.

Because somebody rang it. And the boy who never makes a mistake rang it badly.

THE WINTER TERM is a dark academia mystery about scholarship, belonging, and the price of a place. Every clue is on the page from chapter one: the laundry docket, the padlock, the rota, the letter that types its e a half line high. You can get there before Nell does.

The hardest part is not working out what happened to Cassia Brandt.

It is what they offer Nell to keep it.
