Titolo: Proof of Murder
Autore: Emma Hartwell
Sottotitolo: A Cozy Bakery Mystery of Secrets, Sabotage, and a Killer Recipe
Nicchia/keyword: cozy mystery bakery small town

Descrizione (back cover):
Maggie Doyle came home to save her mother's bakery, not to solve a murder.

Six months after her mother's death, Maggie has poured every dollar and every ounce of hope into reopening Sweetbriar Bakery before Founder's Day, the one morning the whole town turns out. But when she opens the proofing drawer to check her sourdough, she finds Harold Pruitt, president of the Founder's Day Committee, stone cold and very dead.

The sheriff's suspicion turns to certainty when he pulls a recipe card from Harold's pocket, written in handwriting Maggie would know anywhere: her mother's.

Now the whole town is asking the same question. Did Maggie Doyle come home to bake, or to finish something her mother started?

With her business, her reputation, and possibly her freedom on the line, Maggie has one week to prove her innocence before Founder's Day ends in handcuffs instead of a ribbon cutting. Between batches of cinnamon rolls and increasingly uncomfortable visits from the sheriff, she starts digging through the town's oldest secrets and discovers that Pruitt was not nearly as beloved as his obituary will claim.

Everyone in Maple Hollow has a motive. Everyone has a recipe box full of secrets. And somebody is willing to let Maggie take the fall for a murder that started long before she ever came home.

Perfect for fans of small town bakeries, found family, and mysteries with a side of frosting.
