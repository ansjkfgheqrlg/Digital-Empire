Titolo: The Ninth Winter
Autore: Rebecca Miller
Sottotitolo: An Amish Suspense Novel
Nicchia/keyword: amish romance suspense

Descrizione (back cover):
Nine years ago, Sarah Yoder drowned in a flooded quarry. The county called it an accident. Her sister has counted every day since.

Rebecca Yoder is a midwife in Willow Creek, Pennsylvania — steady hands, small handwriting, nine years of other people's births set down in a ledger she has never let out of her sight. She has stopped expecting anyone to explain the night her sister died.

Then she finds Sarah's own notebook, hidden where nobody was meant to look, and reads eleven pages of dates, initials and figures in her sister's hand.

Sarah had been keeping a record too.

What she was writing down would ruin a man who is still in his office, still signing checks, still shaking hands after the Sunday meal. Proving it means asking questions the district would rather were never asked — of men who cannot afford to answer, about a girl everyone had quietly agreed to stop discussing.

And it means trusting Daniel Beiler, who left Willow Creek nine years ago without a word, and has come back the winter the ice broke open.

Some silences are not peace. They are only what is left when nobody will say the name out loud.

THE NINTH WINTER is a slow-burning Amish suspense novel about grief, complicity, and what it costs to tell the truth in a community built on keeping quiet. Clean, character-driven and quietly devastating — for readers of Amish mystery, small-town suspense and stories where the hardest thing to face is your own people.
