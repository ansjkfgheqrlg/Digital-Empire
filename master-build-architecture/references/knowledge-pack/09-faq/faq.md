# FAQ

## Why workflow first?
It prevents building screens or APIs with no validated user/business flow.

## Why many agents?
Roles are activated by risk; they are not all run by default.

## Can the system self-modify?
It can propose bounded, evidence-backed changes. SUP approves material changes.

## What counts as proof?
A reproducible command, reviewed artefact, or traceable source—not a claim.
