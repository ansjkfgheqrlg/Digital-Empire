## Overview

The Market Content Analysis Subagent is a specialized AI tool that evaluates website content for marketing effectiveness, copy quality, and persuasion power. It analyzes messaging clarity, value proposition strength, and call-to-action effectiveness.

## Purpose

This agent performs comprehensive content audits by:
- Analyzing headline clarity and messaging effectiveness
- Evaluating value proposition differentiation and proof
- Assessing copy persuasion techniques and emotional appeal
- Measuring content depth and educational value
- Scoring call-to-action placement and effectiveness

## Key Components

### Content Evaluation Framework
- **Headline Clarity** (0-10): Communication clarity and specificity
- **Value Proposition** (0-10): Differentiation and proven outcomes
- **Copy Persuasion** (0-10): Benefits vs features, emotional triggers, objection handling
- **Content Depth** (0-10): Educational coverage and comprehensiveness
- **CTA Effectiveness** (0-10): Action-orientation and placement strategy

### Analysis Process
1. Fetch key pages (homepage, about, pricing, features, blog)
2. Evaluate each content dimension with 0-10 scoring
3. Identify specific issues with before/after examples
4. Generate rewriting suggestions with rationale
5. Provide actionable recommendations for improvement

## How It Applies to Digital Empire

The Content Analysis agent identifies opportunities for:
- **CRO/Copy/Funnel Service**: Clients with weak messaging or poor copy persuasion
- Messaging optimization projects and copywriting engagements
- Content strategy consulting

## Output Format

Generates structured analysis with:
- Overall content score (0-10)
- Dimension-by-dimension breakdown
- Top wins and critical fixes
- Before/after copy examples with explanations
- Specific improvement recommendations
