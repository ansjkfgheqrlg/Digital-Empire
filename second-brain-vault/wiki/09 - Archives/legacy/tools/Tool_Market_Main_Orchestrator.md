## Overview

The Market Main Orchestrator is the central command system for the Digital Empire marketing automation suite. It coordinates 5 parallel marketing analysis subagents to perform comprehensive website audits and marketing assessments.

## Purpose

The Market Orchestrator launches and manages:
- Market Competitive Intelligence Subagent
- Market Content Analysis Subagent
- Market Conversion/CRO Subagent
- Market Strategy & Positioning Subagent
- Market Technical SEO Subagent

These agents work in parallel to deliver a complete 360-degree marketing audit.

## Key Components

### Parallel Subagent System
- **Competitive Analysis**: Identifies positioning gaps and market opportunities
- **Content Strategy**: Evaluates content quality, authority, and SEO readiness
- **Conversion Optimization**: Analyzes funnel performance and CRO opportunities
- **Strategic Positioning**: Defines core messaging and value propositions
- **Technical Foundation**: Assesses SEO, performance, and technical health

### Integration Points
- Launches from `/market audit` slash command
- Combines reports from all 5 agents into unified audit output
- Provides actionable recommendations across all marketing dimensions

## How It Applies to Digital Empire

The Market Orchestrator is the primary tool for qualifying potential clients and identifying service opportunities:
- **CRO/Copy/Funnel Service**: Highlights websites with poor conversion funnels
- **AI Implementation Service**: Identifies businesses with process automation opportunities
