## Overview

The Market Strategy & Positioning Subagent is a marketing strategy specialist that evaluates overall marketing strategy, growth opportunities, pricing effectiveness, and revenue optimization potential.

## Purpose

This agent performs strategic assessments by:
- Evaluating brand consistency and trust architecture
- Assessing authority signals and thought leadership
- Analyzing pricing strategy and market positioning
- Identifying acquisition channel maturity
- Evaluating retention and expansion potential
- Identifying quick wins and growth opportunities

## Key Components

### Strategic Assessment Framework
- **Brand Consistency** (0-10): Visual consistency, messaging alignment, design quality
- **Trust Architecture** (0-10): About page quality, contact accessibility, social proof, certifications
- **Authority Signals** (0-10): Thought leadership, media mentions, awards, community presence
- **Pricing Strategy** (0-10): Transparency, trial options, tier structure, upsell paths
- **Acquisition Channels** (0-10): Channel diversity, content maturity, SEO, social, paid, partnerships
- **Retention & Expansion** (0-10): Onboarding, community, upgrade paths, communication, documentation

### Growth Opportunity Framework
- **Quick Revenue Wins** (1-2 weeks): Pricing optimization, CTA improvements, social proof, urgency
- **Medium-Term Growth** (1-3 months): Content expansion, email nurture, positioning pages, referral programs
- **Long-Term Growth** (3+ months): SEO dominance, partnership development, product expansion, authority building

## How It Applies to Digital Empire

The Strategy & Positioning agent identifies:
- **CRO/Copy/Funnel Service**: Full-funnel growth opportunities and optimization potential
- **AI Implementation Service**: Process automation and operational efficiency opportunities
- Revenue growth potential and strategic recommendations for clients

## Output Format

Generates comprehensive strategic analysis with:
- Overall strategy score (0-10)
- Brand and trust assessment with scores
- Growth strategy evaluation across pricing, acquisition, retention
- Identified revenue opportunities by timeline
- Specific strategic recommendations
- Growth playbook outline
