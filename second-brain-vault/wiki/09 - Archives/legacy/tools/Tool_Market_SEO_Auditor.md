## Overview

The Market SEO Auditor is a specialized tool for conducting comprehensive SEO audits covering on-page optimization, technical SEO, content quality, and competitive positioning.

## Purpose

This tool performs SEO audits by:
- Analyzing on-page SEO elements (titles, meta descriptions, headers)
- Evaluating technical SEO (crawlability, indexability, performance)
- Assessing content quality and relevance
- Analyzing competitor SEO strategies
- Identifying keyword opportunities
- Generating prioritized improvement roadmap

## Key Components

### On-Page SEO Analysis
- Title tag optimization (length, keywords, uniqueness)
- Meta description quality and click-worthiness
- H1-H6 hierarchy and keyword usage
- Content quality and length
- Image alt text and optimization
- Internal linking strategy

### Technical SEO Assessment
- Site structure and crawlability
- XML sitemap presence and quality
- Robots.txt configuration
- Page speed and Core Web Vitals
- Mobile responsiveness
- HTTPS implementation
- Structured data/schema markup

### Content Quality Evaluation
- Keyword relevance and density
- Content depth and comprehensiveness
- E-E-A-T signals (expertise, experience, authority, trustworthiness)
- Content freshness and updates
- Readability and user experience

### Competitive Analysis
- Top 10 competitor analysis
- Keyword positioning vs competitors
- Content gaps and opportunities
- Backlink profile assessment
- Featured snippet opportunities

## How It Applies to Digital Empire

The SEO Auditor applies to:
- **Client Services**: SEO audits for CRO/Copy/Funnel service
- **Lead Qualification**: SEO needs identification for service offering
- **Own Optimization**: Improving Digital Empire's organic visibility
- **Technical Assessment**: Identifying SEO barriers to client success

## Output Format

Generates:
- Comprehensive SEO audit report
- Technical SEO score (0-100)
- On-page optimization score
- Content quality assessment
- Competitive positioning analysis
- Top 20 improvement opportunities
- Keyword research and targeting
- Implementation roadmap
- Timeline for improvements
