## Overview

The Market Conversion Optimization Subagent is a CRO specialist that analyzes websites for conversion barriers, friction points, and optimization opportunities across the entire user journey.

## Purpose

This agent performs conversion audits by:
- Mapping primary conversion paths from awareness to purchase
- Evaluating CTA strategy and placement effectiveness
- Assessing social proof strength and credibility
- Analyzing friction points in the user journey
- Identifying trust signals and urgency/scarcity elements
- Generating testable A/B optimization hypotheses

## Key Components

### CRO Evaluation Framework
- **CTA Strategy** (0-10): Button clarity, text quality, placement, visual hierarchy
- **Social Proof** (0-10): Testimonials, logos, case studies, numbers, third-party reviews
- **Friction Analysis** (0-10): Conversion steps, form fields, account requirements, speed
- **Trust Signals** (0-10): Security badges, privacy, guarantees, contact accessibility
- **Urgency & Scarcity** (0-10): Authentic urgency elements and scarcity messaging

### Analysis Process
1. Map conversion path (homepage → features → pricing → signup)
2. Evaluate each CRO dimension with 0-10 scoring
3. Detect funnel leak points and severity assessment
4. Generate A/B test hypotheses for critical issues
5. Estimate revenue impact of fixes
6. Prioritize recommendations by impact and effort

## How It Applies to Digital Empire

The Conversion Optimization agent identifies:
- **CRO/Copy/Funnel Service**: Primary target client segment with conversion issues
- Friction points and quick-win optimization opportunities
- Revenue impact of specific improvements
- A/B testing opportunities for clients

## Output Format

Generates comprehensive CRO analysis with:
- Overall conversion score (0-10)
- Dimension-by-dimension scoring
- Conversion path mapping
- Funnel leak detection and severity assessment
- A/B test hypothesis suggestions
- Specific optimization recommendations
- Revenue impact estimates
