## Project Overview

YouTube Lead Magnet Strategy is a comprehensive framework for using YouTube as a lead generation channel. The program teaches entrepreneurs how to build organic reach, establish authority, and drive high-quality leads to paid products.

**Format**: Video series, implementation guides, analytics framework
**Language**: Italian
**Status**: Content Curation Phase
**Target Audience**: Digital entrepreneurs, product creators
**Reference Videos**: 8 curated YouTube videos

## Problem Solved

**"I don't know how to get people interested in my products without spending money on ads"**

This strategy addresses:
1. Low organic reach and visibility
2. Difficulty establishing authority without audience
3. Confusion about YouTube content strategy
4. Lead generation without budget
5. Converting viewers to email subscribers

After implementing YouTube Lead Magnet strategy, students will:

## Platform Specifics

### YouTube Algorithm Optimization
- Video length: 12-25 minutes (optimal engagement)
- Consistency: 2-3 videos weekly (channel authority)
- Thumbnails: Custom, high contrast, face or text-based
- Titles: Keyword-rich, emotional trigger, benefit-driven
- Tags: Mix of broad and specific keywords

### End Screens
- Subscribe button (prominently placed)
- Lead magnet link in description
- Related video recommendations
- Playlist recommendations

### Call-to-Action Placement
- Intro: "Subscribe for X" (first 20 seconds)
- Middle: Lead magnet mention (natural integration)
- Outro: "Get your free guide in description" (10-15 seconds)
- Pinned comment: Lead magnet link + offer
