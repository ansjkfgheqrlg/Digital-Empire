## Digital Empire Client Acquisition Funnel

### Funnel Overview
Five-step funnel optimized for acquiring CRO sprint clients through education, lead magnets, VSL (Video Sales Letter), qualification, and strategy calls.

---

## Step 1: AWARENESS (Top of Funnel)

**Goal**: Reach decision-makers with education about conversion optimization

**Channels**:
- LinkedIn content (organic posts)
- YouTube videos
- Blog articles
- Cold traffic ads

**Content Strategy**:
- **Educational Focus**: "You're losing conversions and don't know it"
- **Formats**: Posts, short videos, carousels
- **Key Messages**: 
  - Hidden conversion leaks in funnels
  - Cost of poor conversion rate
  - Why standard tactics fail

**Key Metrics**: Reach, impressions, click-through rate

---

## Step 2: INTEREST (Middle of Funnel)

**Goal**: Capture contact and establish expertise

**Lead Magnets**:
- "Free CRO Audit Template"
- "Conversion Optimization Checklist"
- Topic: "The 5 CRO Mistakes Costing You €X/month"

**Educational Content**:
- Webinars/Workshops on CRO fundamentals
- Free training videos
- Case study snapshots

**Nurturing Sequence**:
- 5-7 email sequence building authority
- Educational content (no selling)
- Builds trust and credibility

**Key Metrics**: Lead capture rate, email open rate, click rate

---

## Step 3: CONSIDERATION (Bottom of Funnel)

**Application Page**: 
Qualification form (NOT generic "contact us")

**7 Key Questions**:
1. Name and company
2. Company website
3. Main product/service
4. Monthly website traffic (range)
5. Current conversion rate (estimate)
6. Monthly ad spend (range)
7. Primary objective for next 90 days

**Video Sales Letter (5-10 minutes)**:

**VSL Structure**:
1. **Hook** (1-2 min): Specific result achieved for similar client
2. **Problem** (1-2 min): Why conversions aren't improving (3 specific reasons)
3. **Solution** (2 min): The Conversion Sprint method and philosophy
4. **Proof** (2-3 min): 2-3 case studies with specific numbers
5. **Offer** (1 min): What's included in the sprint package
6. **CTA** (30 sec): Clear call to complete application (5-7 qualifying questions)

**Application Submission**:
Detailed qualification form on separate page

**Key Metrics**: VSL completion rate, application submission rate, form abandonment points

---

## Step 4: DECISION (Strategy Call)

**Duration**: 30-45 minutes | **Owner**: Founder

**Call Structure**:

1. **Discovery Phase** (15 min)
   - Current situation assessment
   - Business goals and objectives
   - What they've already tried
   - Current metrics and pain points

2. **Diagnosis Phase** (10 min)
   - Preliminary analysis based on application
   - Identification of top 3 optimization opportunities
   - Explanation of why previous approaches didn't work

3. **Sprint Presentation** (10 min)
   - What Digital Empire does differently
   - Sprint methodology overview
   - Timeline and deliverables
   - Working style and expectations

4. **Next Steps** (5 min)
   - Pricing structure (setup fee + variable on uplift)
   - Contract process and timeline
   - When sprint can start
   - Answer remaining questions

**Decision Point**:
- IF 3+ ICP criteria met → Propose contract and move to onboarding
- IF 2- ICP criteria met → Decline gracefully with free resource

---

## Step 5: ONBOARDING (After Sale)

**Immediate Actions**:
1. Contract signing and setup fee payment
2. Onboarding call (Phase 2 Briefing - 40 questions)
3. Credential sharing (analytics, ads manager, platform access)
4. Sprint timeline sharing and expectations setting

**Transition to Phase 2**:
- Begin 6-phase sprint process
- Assign dedicated sprint timeline
- Set baseline metrics and success criteria

## Application Page Routing Logic

### Post-Application Qualification Rules

**Route 1: Qualified (Monthly Traffic >5K + Ad Spend >€1K)**
→ Automatically scheduled strategy call
→ Email: "Thanks! Your call is scheduled for [date/time]"

**Route 2: Insufficient Traffic (<5K/month)**
→ Email: "Great application! We're not the perfect fit right now, but here's what to do first..."
→ Free resource: "How to Get Your First 5K Monthly Visitors"
→ Invitation to reconnect in 6 months

**Route 3: No Validated Offer**
→ Email: "Your situation is common. Before optimization, you need offer validation..."
→ Free resource: "How to Validate Your Offer Before Scaling"
→ Invitation to reconnect when offer is generating sales

**Route 4: Other Issues**
→ Manual review by founder
→ Personalized response with guidance

## Friction Points to Monitor

| Friction Point | Metric | Target | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Landing → Application Form | Drop-off % | <30% | Optimize page, reduce friction |
| Form Submission → Call Booked | Conversion % | >80% | Auto-qualify/manual routing |
| Call Booked → Call Attended | Show-up rate | >85% | Reminder sequence, time zone check |
| Strategy Call → Client Signed | Close rate | >30% | Call script refinement |
| Application → Disqualified | Ratio | <20% | Check ICP definition |

## Key Success Factors

1. **Crystal Clear ICP**: Application questions must accurately identify ideal clients
2. **Strong VSL**: 5-10 minute video must build credibility and desire
3. **Smooth Routing**: Disqualified leads must receive helpful guidance
4. **Fast Response**: Strategy call should be within 24-48 hours of application
5. **Clear Process**: Prospect knows exactly what happens next at every stage
