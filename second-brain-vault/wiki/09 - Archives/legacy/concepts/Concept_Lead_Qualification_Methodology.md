## Overview

The Lead Qualification Methodology is a systematic framework for evaluating prospect fit and readiness for conversion. It uses weighted scoring to prioritize outreach efforts toward highest-potential leads.

## Core Principles

### Qualification vs Lead Quality
- **Qualification**: Assesses fit with service offering
- **Lead Quality**: Measures likelihood to convert and value

### Multi-Source Scoring
Different lead sources have different scoring frameworks:
- **No Website Leads** (WF-A): Business fundamentals + engagement
- **Poor Funnel Leads** (WF-B): Ad activity + conversion readiness
- **AI Implementation Leads** (WF-F): Process automation potential + company structure

## Service 1: CRO/Copy/Funnel Qualification

### No Website Lead Scoring
| Criterion | Points | Rationale |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Rating >= 4.0 | +20 | Indicates established business |
| 50+ Reviews | +20 | Proof of customer base and reviews |
| High demand category | +15 | Hairdresser, dental, restaurant |
| Email found | +10 | Easier to reach decision maker |
| Phone available | +5 | Alternative contact method |
| **Target: 60+ = Qualified** | |

### Poor Funnel Lead Scoring
| Criterion | Points | Rationale |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| 3+ active ads | +15 | Shows marketing commitment |
| Email available | +15 | Contact information priority |
| Phone available | +10 | Secondary contact method |
| High demand category | +10 | Industry fit |
| Conversion score < 5/10 | +variable | Funnel improvement potential |
| **Target: 60+ = Qualified** | |

## Service 2: AI Implementation Qualification

### AI Implementation Lead Scoring
| Criterion | Points | Rationale |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| AI potential score >= 60 | +30 | Strong automation opportunity |
| Manual job postings found | +20 | Indicates repetitive work |
| Decision maker identified | +20 | Revenue-ready lead |
| 20-50 employees (sweet spot) | +10 | Ideal implementation size |
| Email/Phone available | +15/+10 | Contact efficiency |
| AI-priority industry | +10 | Sector fit for solutions |
| **Target: 70+ = Qualified** | |

## Band Classification

### Standard Band System
- **Band A** (70-100): Hot lead, contact immediately
- **Band B** (40-69): Valid lead, include in next batch
- **Band C** (0-39): Weak lead, archive for future

### Outreach Readiness Criteria
A lead is ready for outreach when:
- [ ] Has email OR phone contact
- [ ] Qualifies in Band A or B
- [ ] Has not been previously contacted
- [ ] No exclusion criteria apply

## Application to Digital Empire

### How to Qualify Each Service Line

**CRO/Copy/Funnel Service** → Target:
- Businesses with no web presence (WF-A)
- Businesses running ads but converting poorly (WF-B)
- Local SMEs seeking growth and professionalization

**AI Implementation Service** → Target:
- Companies 10+ employees with visible processes (WF-F)
- Industries with high manual work (support, back office, reporting)
- Growth-stage companies seeking operational efficiency
