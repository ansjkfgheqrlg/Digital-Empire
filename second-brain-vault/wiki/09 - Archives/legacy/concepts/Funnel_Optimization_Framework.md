**Objective**: Convert visitors to email subscribers

**Elements to Optimize**:

- Landing page headline
- Copy clarity
- Visual design
- Form fields (fewer = higher conversion)
- Loading speed
- Mobile responsiveness

- Visitors: 1,000/month
- Conversion rate: 10%
- Leads captured: 100/month

**Optimization Target**:
- Conversion rate: 20%
- Leads captured: 200/month

**Optimization Tactics**:
1. **Headline test** (5 variations)
   - A: "The Complete Claude Code Manual"
   - B: "Learn Claude Code: From Zero to €10K/Month"

4. **Design testing**

   - Color contrast

   - Scarcity messaging

### Level 3: Email List Nurture
**Objective**: Warm the list and build trust

**Elements to Optimize**:
- Email frequency (1-3x per week)
- Email timing (days/times)
- Email copy quality
- Subject line effectiveness
- Link placement
- CTA clarity
- Segmentation

**Current State Estimate**:

- Segment 1 (Free content interested): 120

- Email click rate: 8%

**Optimization Targets**:
- Email open rate: 35-40%
- Email click rate: 12-15%

**Optimization Tactics**:
1. **Subject line testing** (Highest impact lever)

3. **Copy framework optimization**
   - Story-based vs. benefit-based

   - Urgency vs. no urgency

4. **Segmentation strategy**
   - Engaged vs. cold subscribers
   - Interested in courses vs. agencies vs. freelancing
   - Previous purchasers vs. non-purchasers

## The A/B Testing Framework

### Testing Approach
```
HYPOTHESIS → TEST → MEASURE → LEARN → IMPLEMENT

Example:
HYPOTHESIS: Stronger guarantee will increase sales conversion 25%
TEST: Add "30-day money-back guarantee" to sales page
MEASURE: 100 visitors, compare conversion A (no guarantee) vs B (guarantee)
LEARN: Guarantee increased conversions from 2% to 2.8% (+40%)
IMPLEMENT: Keep guarantee on all future pages
```

### Statistical Significance
- Minimum 100 visitors per variation
- Run test until clear winner
- Document all results
- Iterate continuously

### Testing Priority (Highest ROI)
1. **Headlines** (typically 30-50% impact)
2. **Primary CTA copy** (20-40% impact)
3. **Lead magnet quality** (15-30% impact)
4. **Social proof** (10-20% impact)
5. **Body copy** (5-15% impact)
