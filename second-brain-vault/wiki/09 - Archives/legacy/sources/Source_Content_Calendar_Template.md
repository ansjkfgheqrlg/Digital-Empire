## Overview

The Content Calendar Template is a structured planning tool for organizing and scheduling social media content, blog posts, email newsletters, and other marketing content across channels and timeframes.

## Calendar Structure

### Minimum Information Per Post
- **Date & Time**: When to publish
- **Channel**: Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, Blog, Email
- **Format**: Image, Video, Text, Poll, Carousel
- **Copy/Title**: Post content and headline
- **CTAs**: Specific call-to-action
- **Link/Assets**: URL or asset file
- **Status**: Draft, Scheduled, Published

### Monthly View Benefits
- See at a glance: content distribution and balance
- Identify gaps or over-saturation
- Plan for seasonal events or campaigns
- Assign content to team members
- Track publishing schedule consistency

### Weekly Planning Benefits
- Detailed planning with variations
- Account for trending topics
- Respond to customer feedback
- Test different post types and times
- Optimize engagement based on metrics

## Content Types & Frequency

### Digital Empire Social Content Mix

**LinkedIn** (B2B CRO & AI Implementation)
- Frequency: 3-4x per week
- Content: Case studies, industry insights, thought leadership
- Best times: Tuesday-Thursday, 8am-10am, 12pm-1pm

**Instagram** (Design, lifestyle, behind-scenes)
- Frequency: 4-5x per week (stories and posts)
- Content: Before/afters, team, design process, tips
- Best times: Tuesday-Thursday, 7-9pm

**Email Newsletter** (Subscriber nurture)
- Frequency: 1-2x per week
- Content: Case studies, tips, exclusive insights
- Best times: Tuesday-Thursday, 8am

**Blog** (SEO and thought leadership)
- Frequency: 2x per week
- Content: Deep-dive guides, case studies, industry analysis
- Format: 1500-2500 word articles

## Publishing Schedule Template

### Daily Schedule Recommendation
- **Monday-Friday**: 1 post per platform (5 posts/week)
- **Weekends**: 1-2 stories or lighter content
- **Email**: Tuesday + Thursday 8am

### Platform-Specific Timing
**LinkedIn**: 8-10am, 12pm-1pm (M-F)
**Instagram**: 7-9pm, 11am (M-F)
**Twitter**: 9am, 1pm, 5pm (M-F)
**Email**: 8am Tuesday, Thursday

## Metrics to Track

- **Impressions**: Reach of each post
- **Engagement**: Likes, comments, shares
- **Click-through**: Links clicked
- **Conversions**: Leads or sales attributed
- **Growth**: Followers/subscribers gained
- **Sentiment**: Positive vs negative engagement
