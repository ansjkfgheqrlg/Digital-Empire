## Operating Model Overview

### The 6-Phase Sprint Process
Every client engagement follows this proven structure:

1. **Phase 1: Qualification** - Is this an ICP client?
2. **Phase 2: Briefing** - Gather context with 40 questions
3. **Phase 3: Research** - Deep dive into target audience
4. **Phase 4: Diagnosis & Strategy** - Create action plan
5. **Phase 5: Implementation & Copy** - Execute with APP-SOC framework
6. **Phase 6: Measurement & Handover** - Deliver results and playbook

[[Digital_Empire_6_Phase_Process]]

### The Client Acquisition Funnel
Prospects journey through five distinct stages:

1. **Awareness** - Educational content reaches decision-makers
2. **Interest** - Lead magnet captures contact and builds trust
3. **Consideration** - VSL video and application qualify prospects
4. **Decision** - Strategy call determines fit and value
5. **Onboarding** - Briefing and sprint begin

[[Digital_Empire_Client_Acquisition_Funnel]]
