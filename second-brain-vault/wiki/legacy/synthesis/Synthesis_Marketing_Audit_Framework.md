## Overview

The Marketing Audit Framework is a comprehensive analysis system that evaluates websites across six dimensions using parallel subagents. It provides a complete 360-degree assessment of marketing effectiveness with a composite marketing score and actionable recommendations.

## Six Dimensions of Marketing Excellence

### 1. Content & Messaging (25% weight)
**What It Measures**:
- Copy quality and clarity
- Value proposition strength
- Persuasion techniques and emotional appeal
- Content depth and educational value
- Call-to-action effectiveness

**Tools**: [[Tool_Market_Content_Analysis]]

**Key Questions**:
- Is the headline clear and specific?
- Is the value proposition differentiated?
- Does copy focus on benefits or features?
- Are CTAs compelling and action-oriented?
- Is there enough content for purchase decisions?

### 2. Conversion Optimization (20% weight)
**What It Measures**:
- CTA strategy and placement
- Social proof strength
- Friction points in conversion path
- Trust signals and security
- Urgency and scarcity elements

**Tools**: [[Tool_Market_Conversion_Optimization]]

**Key Questions**:
- Is the conversion path smooth and frictionless?
- Are CTAs clear and benefit-driven?
- Is there compelling social proof?
- Are trust signals present?
- Does urgency feel authentic?

### 3. SEO & Discoverability (20% weight)
**What It Measures**:
- Technical SEO elements
- Content architecture and organization
- Page speed and mobile readiness
- Crawlability and indexability
- Schema markup and structured data

**Tools**: [[Tool_Market_Technical_SEO]]

**Key Questions**:
- Is the site technically sound?
- Can search engines crawl and index it?
- Is content well-organized?
- Is the mobile experience optimized?
- Are there ranking signals present?

### 4. Competitive Positioning (15% weight)
**What It Measures**:
- Positioning clarity vs competitors
- Market awareness and differentiation
- Feature messaging gaps
- Alternative page opportunities
- Competitive market awareness

**Tools**: [[Tool_Market_Competitive_Intelligence]]

**Key Questions**:
- Can visitors distinguish them from competitors?
- Are they aware of alternatives?
- Do they position against competitors?
- What positioning gaps exist?
- Are there untapped market opportunities?

### 5. Brand & Trust (10% weight)
**What It Measures**:
- Brand consistency across pages
- Trust architecture (about page, contact, credentials)
- Authority signals and thought leadership
- Professional design quality
- Mission and values clarity

**Tools**: [[Tool_Market_Strategy_Positioning]]

**Key Questions**:
- Is the brand consistent?
- Are trust signals present?
- Is there evidence of authority?
- Is the design professional?
- Is the team credible?

### 6. Growth & Strategy (10% weight)
**What It Measures**:
- Pricing strategy and transparency
- Acquisition channel maturity
- Retention and expansion strategy
- Revenue optimization potential
- Growth opportunities

**Tools**: [[Tool_Market_Strategy_Positioning]]

**Key Questions**:
- Is pricing transparent and competitive?
- Are multiple acquisition channels active?
- Is there a retention strategy?
- Are upsell opportunities present?
- What are the growth bottlenecks?

## Scoring Methodology

### Composite Marketing Score (0-100)
```
Final Score = (Content × 0.25) + (Conversion × 0.20) + (SEO × 0.20) + 
              (Competitive × 0.15) + (Brand × 0.10) + (Growth × 0.10)
```

### Score Interpretation

| Score | Assessment | Action |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| 80-100 | Excellent | Maintain and optimize |
| 60-79 | Good | Address key gaps |
| 40-59 | Fair | Significant work needed |
| 0-39 | Poor | Major overhaul required |

## Audit Process Flow

### Stage 1: Parallel Analysis (24 hours)
5 subagents analyze simultaneously:
- Market-content → Content scoring
- Market-conversion → CRO scoring
- Market-technical → SEO scoring
- market-competitive → Positioning scoring
- market-strategy → Brand & Growth scoring

### Stage 2: Report Synthesis
- Compile individual scores into dimensions
- Calculate weighted composite score
- Identify top 5-10 opportunities
- Rank by impact and ease of implementation

### Stage 3: Recommendation Development
- Quick wins (1-2 weeks)
- Medium-term improvements (1-3 months)
- Long-term strategic initiatives (3+ months)
- Resource requirements and timeline

## Audit Output Components

Each audit includes:
1. **Executive Summary** (1 page)
   - Overall score and assessment
   - Top 3 opportunities
   - Recommended next steps

2. **Dimension Scores** (1 page)
   - Visual score breakdown
   - Key findings per dimension
   - Comparison to industry benchmarks

3. **Detailed Analysis** (15-20 pages)
   - Full dimension breakdowns
   - Specific findings and examples
   - Before/after improvement opportunities
   - Implementation recommendations

4. **Opportunity Roadmap**
   - Quick wins (immediate)
   - Medium-term priorities (1-3 months)
   - Long-term strategy (3+ months)
   - ROI estimates for each

5. **Data Tables & Scorecards**
   - Detailed scoring by element
   - Competitor comparison matrix
   - Keyword opportunity list
   - Content gap analysis
