## Overview

The Email Nurture Sequence Template provides frameworks for multi-email campaigns that guide prospects from initial interest through qualification to sales-ready status.

## Key Principles

1. **Value Before Ask**: Lead emails educate and solve problems before requesting a meeting
2. **Progressive Engagement**: Increase complexity and commitment requests as engagement grows
3. **Segmented Content**: Different messages for different engagement levels
4. **Clear Timeline**: Consistent sending schedule (day 1, 3, 7, 14, 21)
5. **Measurable Engagement**: Track opens, clicks, and replies to assess readiness

## Nurture Sequence Framework (7 Emails)

### Email 1: Welcome + Situation Recognition
- Acknowledge their interest or download
- Show you understand their specific situation
- CTA: Introduce yourself or next resource

### Email 2: Value Content (Day 3)
- Share educational content relevant to their challenge
- Could be: guide, case study, video, article
- CTA: Learn more or check resource

### Email 3: Problem Validation (Day 7)
- Deepen empathy by acknowledging their struggle
- Share statistics or common patterns
- Offer perspective shift
- CTA: Consider this approach

### Email 4: Solution Introduction (Day 14)
- Introduce your approach/service
- Explain why it's different from alternatives
- Include social proof
- CTA: See how it works or book 15-min call

### Email 5: Objection Handler (Day 21)
- Address most common objection
- Provide alternative/simplified approach
- CTA: Try low-friction option or book call

### Email 6: Urgency Element (Day 28)
- Introduce time-sensitive element
- Could be: pricing change, limited spots, seasonal opportunity
- CTA: Claim spot or schedule call

### Email 7: Final Ask (Day 35)
- Direct proposal: schedule sales call
- If no engagement: move to win-back sequence
- CTA: Book 30-min consultation

## Segmentation Logic

### High Engagement Track (Open 3+, Click 2+)
- Shorter intervals (every 3 days)
- Direct asks in emails 4-5
- Offer demo/consultation
- Higher-touch follow-up

### Medium Engagement Track (Open 2, Click 0)
- Standard intervals (every 7 days)
- More content/education-focused
- Gentle CTA toward consultation
- Standard follow-up

### Low Engagement Track (Open 0-1)
- Extend intervals to 10-14 days
- Simplify message, focus on biggest benefit
- Offer alternative CTAs (attend webinar, read case study)
- Consider pausing after email 5
