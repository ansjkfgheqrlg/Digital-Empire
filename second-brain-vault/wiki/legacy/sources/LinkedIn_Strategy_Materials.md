### Implementation Framework

#### Phase 1: Optimization
- Profile optimization (headline, about, featured content)
- Content strategy definition (3-4 pillars)
- Audience research and targeting

#### Phase 2: Content Creation
- Weekly content calendar (4-5 posts/week minimum)
- 600 comments/week schedule
- Engagement in target accounts

#### Phase 3: Lead Generation
- 100 cold DMs/week to qualified prospects
- Personalized messaging (not templated)
- Value-first approach to outreach

- Strategy calls from interested prospects
- Application process for clients
- Sales process refinement
