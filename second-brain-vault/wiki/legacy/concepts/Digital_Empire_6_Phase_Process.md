## Digital Empire 6-Phase Operating Process

### Overview
Complete sequential process for executing CRO sprints with standardized deliverables, clear accountability, and measurable outcomes.

---

## Phase 1: QUALIFICAZIONE (Qualification)

**Input**: Lead/prospect from funnel, referral, or outreach
**Duration**: 1 strategy call (30-45 min)
**Owner**: Founder
**Tool**: Strategy Call Script, Application Page

### Qualification Criteria (5 Required)
1. **Existing traffic?** (ads or organic, >5K visits/month)
2. **Validated market offer?** (existing sales)
3. **Willing to track and test?** (analytics access)
4. **Test ads budget?** (if applicable, >€1K/month)
5. **Clear decision maker?** (direct access)

### Decision Logic
- **If NO to 2+ criteria** → NOT ICP. Decline with useful resource.
- **If YES to 3+ criteria** → Propose Sprint. Move to Phase 2.

**Deliverable**: Decision (go/no-go) + signed contract

---

## Phase 2: BRIEFING (Information Gathering)

**Input**: Confirmed client with signed contract
**Duration**: 1 call (60-90 min) + processing
**Owner**: Founder + "Briefing Master Pro" skill

### Activities
- 40 structured questions across 6 categories
- Collection of access credentials (analytics, ads, platform)
- Baseline KPI identification (CR, CTR, CPA, AOV, LTV)
- Definition of measurable sprint objective

**Deliverable**: Complete Briefing Document
**Tool**: 40-Question Briefing Template

---

## Phase 3: RICERCA (Research)

**Input**: Completed Briefing Document
**Duration**: 2-3 days
**Owner**: Founder + "Client Research Engine" skill

### Activities
- **Audience Research**: YouTube, Reddit, Google, LinkedIn analysis
- **Competitor Research**: Top 3 competitor landing pages study
- **Pain Points Mapping**: Emotional, practical, social, financial dimensions
- **Objections Collection**: Price, trust, timing, alternative, fit objections
- **Tone of Voice Extraction**: Exact words of target audience

**Deliverable**: Structured Research Report
**Tool**: Research Checklist

---

## Phase 4: DIAGNOSI & STRATEGIA (Diagnosis & Strategy)

**Input**: Research Report + Briefing + Funnel access
**Duration**: 2-3 days
**Owner**: Founder

### Activities
- **Comprehensive CRO Audit**: Landing page, checkout, forms, ads analysis
- **Friction Mapping**: Where conversions are being lost
- **Priority Leak Points Identification**:
  - Above-the-fold (headline, hero section)
  - Form/checkout (unnecessary friction)
  - Proof section (trust elements)
  - CTA (clarity and positioning)
- **Prioritized Backlog**: Impact (high/medium/low) × Effort matrix
- **High-Impact Test Selection**: Choose top 3 tests for sprint

**Deliverable**: Strategic Document (NOT a proposal/quote)
**Tools**: Sample proposals, APP-SOC Framework

---

## Phase 5: IMPLEMENTAZIONE & COPY (Implementation & Copywriting)

**Input**: Strategic Document approved by client
**Duration**: 3-5 days
**Owner**: Founder + "CRO Copy Architect" skill

### Activities
- **Copy Writing**: Using APP-SOC framework
- **Layout Implementation**: UX micro-fixes
- **Tracking Setup**: Pixels, events, UTM parameters
- **Test Launch**: Hypothesis → Variation → Metric → Duration

**Deliverables**:
- Implemented copy
- Active tracking
- Test documentation (hypothesis, variation, target metric, duration)

**Tools**: APP-SOC Framework, Sales Page Template

---

## Phase 6: MISURAZIONE & HANDOVER (Measurement & Handover)

**Input**: Test data (minimum 7-14 days)
**Duration**: 1-2 days (analysis) + 1 handover call
**Owner**: Founder

### Activities
- **Data Reading**: Analysis after test period
- **Results Report**: Uplift %, learnings, suggested next tests
- **Success Fee Calculation**: If uplift confirmed
- **Conversion Playbook Delivery**:
  - Remaining backlog with priorities
  - Replicable test templates
  - CRO checklist for future autonomy
- **30-Day Follow-up**: Upsell or referral opportunity

**Deliverables**:
- Sprint Results Report
- Conversion Playbook
- Success Fee Invoice (if applicable)

**Tools**: Report Template, Conversion Playbook Template

## Standard Timeline

| Day | Phase | Main Activity |
|-----|-------|----------------|
| 0 | Contract Signature | Setup fee payment |
| 1-2 | Phase 2: Briefing | Call + document |
| 3-5 | Phase 3: Research | Research report |
| 6-8 | Phase 4: Strategy | Audit + strategic document |
| 8 | Approval | Client approves strategy |
| 9-13 | Phase 5: Implementation | Copy + tracking + test launch |
| 14-28 | Testing Live | Data collection |
| 29-30 | Phase 6: Handover | Report + playbook + call |
| 60 | Follow-up | Upsell or referral |

**Total Sprint Duration**: 4-5 weeks (with 14-day test running period)

### Key Decision Points

1. **End of Phase 1**: Qualification go/no-go
2. **End of Phase 2**: Have we captured client context correctly?
3. **End of Phase 3**: Do we understand target audience deeply?
4. **End of Phase 4**: Has client approved the strategic direction?
5. **End of Phase 5**: Are tests set up correctly with proper tracking?
6. **End of Phase 6**: What's the next iteration or upsell opportunity?

### Success Metrics for Process Compliance

- **Qualification accuracy**: % of clients who complete full sprint
- **Briefing completeness**: All 5 KPI baseline metrics collected
- **Research quality**: Pain points and objections extracted validated by client
- **Strategy approval rate**: % clients approve strategy on first submission
- **Implementation speed**: Days from strategy approval to test launch
- **Handover completion**: % clients receive Conversion Playbook + follow-up call
