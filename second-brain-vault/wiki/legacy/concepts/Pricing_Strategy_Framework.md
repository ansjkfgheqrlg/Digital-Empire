## Overview

Digital Empire employs a value-based pricing model where products are positioned along a ladder of increasing price and sophistication. This framework ensures each product has clear positioning and target audience.

## Pricing Principles

### 1. Value Perception
Each product must articulate clear transformation:
- What problem does it solve?
- What skills does the student acquire?
- What is the career/business impact?

### 2. Target Audience Segmentation
Prices correlate with buying power and commitment level:
- Students/Beginners: €0-50
- Professionals: €100-300
- Entrepreneurs: €500-2000
- Agencies: €5000+

### 3. Social Proof & Funnel Integration
Product pricing is tied to marketing funnel:
- Free lead magnet → Ebook → Course → Membership
- Each stage builds trust before higher investment
