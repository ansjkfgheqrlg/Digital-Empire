## Overview

The Market Competitive Intelligence Subagent is a competitive analysis specialist that researches and analyzes competitive landscapes to identify positioning opportunities, market gaps, and competitive advantages.

## Purpose

This agent performs competitive intelligence by:
- Identifying 3-5 key competitors (direct and aspirational)
- Analyzing target positioning vs competitor positioning
- Extracting competitor strategies and messaging
- Scoring competitive positioning dimensions
- Identifying actionable positioning gaps and opportunities

## Key Components

### Competitive Analysis Framework
- **Positioning Identification**: Competitor research and categorization
- **Positioning Comparison**: Side-by-side messaging and strategy comparison
- **Competitive Scoring**: Messaging clarity, pricing competitiveness, feature messaging, market awareness
- **Opportunity Detection**: Positioning gaps, content gaps, feature messaging opportunities
- **Strategic Recommendations**: Comparison pages, alternative pages, switching narratives

### Analysis Process
1. Identify competitors via web search (alternatives, vs, competitors, best tools)
2. Analyze target website positioning (statement, audience, differentiators, pricing, social proof)
3. Quick-scan top 3 competitors (positioning, pricing, features, social proof, content strategy)
4. Score positioning dimensions 0-10
5. Identify and prioritize positioning gaps and opportunities
6. Recommend specific actions (comparison pages, alternative pages, counter-messaging)

## How It Applies to Digital Empire

The Competitive Intelligence agent identifies:
- **CRO/Copy/Funnel Service**: Clients lacking clear positioning or comparison strategy
- Positioning gaps to address in service delivery
- Opportunities for comparison and alternative page creation

## Output Format

Generates structured competitive analysis including:
- Overall positioning score
- Competitor matrix (name, category, strengths, weaknesses)
- Positioning comparison table
- Dimension scores with key findings
- Specific, actionable opportunity identification
- Recommended next actions
