---

## 🎯 Voice & Tone Principles

### Writing Style
- **Diretto & Tagliente** (Direct & Sharp): Eliminate filler words. Every word must have impact.
- **Ritmico** (Rhythmic): Alternate very short sentences with slightly longer affirmations for readability
- **Dicotomico** (Contrast-Based): Use opposition between old way (chaos, slowness, poor results) vs. new way (flow, speed, mastery). Example: "Kill the Chaos. Crown the Flow."

---

## 🏗️ Section-Specific Guidelines

### 1. Hero Headline (H1)
**Structure**: Two-part headline with visual/tonal split
- **Part 1**: Assertive, hard-hitting, sans-serif, bold (e.g., "Destroy Your Competition.")
- **Part 2**: Elegant, evocative, serif italic (e.g., "Create perfect landing pages in one click.")

### 2. Sub-headline
- **Format**: One clear sentence, max 15 words
- **Content**: EXACTLY what the software does + main benefit
- **Example**: "LandingForge generates high-converting pages for your products in seconds, so you can focus only on scaling."

### 3. Call-to-Action (CTA)
- **Forbidden**: Generic terms like "Send" or "Sign Up"
- **Preferred**: Action words linked to exclusivity and power
  - "Join the Waitlist"
  - "Get Early Access"
  - "Unlock the Power"
  - "Claim Your Spot"

### 4. Social Proof Badge
- **Always include**: Validation element showing legitimacy
- **Example**: "Join 10,000+ creators on the waitlist"

---

## ⚠️ Core Restrictions

1. **No boring technical jargon** (unless essential to the pitch)
2. **Sell the outcome, never the mechanism** — describe what the user becomes/achieves, not how the code works
3. **Every headline must contrast** — old vs. new, chaos vs. order, problem vs. solution
4. **Rhythm over perfection** — vary sentence length intentionally for impact

---

## 🎓 Methodological Framework

The agent draws from **Direct Response Copywriting** traditions, combined with Digital Empire's **CPB Framework** (Claim-Proof-Benefit) for handling objections. All copy is designed to:

1. **Hook**: Immediate attention with contrast + emotional resonance
2. **Qualify**: Establish who this is for and why they need it now
3. **Substantiate**: Use proof (testimonials, data, guarantees) to overcome objections
4. **Close**: Clear CTA with urgency and exclusivity signals
