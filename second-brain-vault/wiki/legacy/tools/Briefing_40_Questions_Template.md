## Briefing Template — 40 Questions in 6 Categories

### Purpose & Usage
Structured discovery tool used in Phase 2 of Digital Empire's 6-phase process. Conducted during first operational call (distinct from sales call).

**Key Principle**: Document CLIENT'S EXACT WORDS — this language will be used for messaging and research.

---

## Category 1: BUSINESS & OFFER (7 Questions)

1. Describe your business in one sentence. What exactly do you do?
2. What's the specific product/service we're working on in this sprint?
3. What's the price point? Are there different tiers/packages?
4. What's your margin on this product/service?
5. What differentiates you from competitors? (in YOUR words)
6. Why should a customer choose you over [main competitor]?
7. Is there a guarantee? If yes, what? If no, why not?

---

## Category 2: TARGET & IDEAL CLIENT (8 Questions)

8. Who is your ideal customer? (demographics: age, gender, role, industry, location)
9. What's their situation BEFORE they contact you? What's happening in their life/business?
10. What's the trigger that makes them search for a solution like yours? What happened?
11. What alternatives have they already tried before you?
12. Why didn't those alternatives work?
13. What's the IDEAL result they want to achieve?
14. How do they measure success? (in their words, not yours)
15. If you had to describe your best customer ever, who are they? Tell me the story.

---

## Category 3: FUNNEL & CURRENT TRAFFIC (7 Questions)

16. Where does your traffic come from today? (% per channel if possible)
17. How much monthly traffic do you get on the page/funnel we're working on?
18. What's your current funnel? Describe every step from first touchpoint to conversion.
19. What platform do you use? (Shopify, WordPress, Webflow, custom, other)
20. Do you have a pixel/tracking installed? What events do you track?
21. Do you use email marketing? If yes, which tool and what sequences do you have active?
22. Do you do retargeting? If yes, on which platforms and with what creative?

---

## Category 4: CURRENT METRICS — BASELINE KPIs (6 Questions)

23. What's your current conversion rate? (if you don't know, that's fine — we'll find out)
24. What's your current CTR on ads? (if applicable)
25. What's your current CPA?
26. What's your AOV (average order value/contract)?
27. What's your customer LTV (lifetime value)?
28. Where do you lose the most people in the funnel? (if known — otherwise we'll analyze)

---

## Category 5: OBJECTIONS & FRICTION (6 Questions)

29. What's the question prospects ask MOST OFTEN before buying?
30. What's the main reason people DON'T buy?
31. What objections do you hear repeated in sales calls or messages?
32. Is there something customers fear might go wrong?
33. How much time passes on average from first contact to purchase?
34. Is there a specific moment in the process where people "disappear" or get stuck?

---

## Category 6: SPRINT OBJECTIVES & EXPECTATIONS (6 Questions)

35. What's the MINIMUM result you'd consider a success for this sprint?
36. What's the IDEAL result (dream)?
37. Have you done A/B testing before? If yes, what did you test and what did you learn?
38. Is there something you've already tried to optimize that didn't work?
39. Are there brand, compliance, or legal constraints we need to respect?
40. Who approves changes? What's the approval process and how fast is it?

---

## Expected Output After Briefing

### Complete Briefing Document containing:

1. **Business Snapshot**: 3-5 line summary of what client does and what we're selling

2. **ICP Profile**: Ideal customer identity, triggers, before/after situation

3. **Funnel Map**: Visual map of current funnel with all steps

4. **KPI Baseline**: Table with current CR, CTR, CPA, AOV, LTV (estimated if needed)

5. **Top 5 Objections**: Prioritized list of most frequent objections

6. **Sprint Objective**: Specific, measurable target metric

7. **Constraints**: Brand, compliance, timeline, approval process

8. **Identified Gaps**: Questions client couldn't answer = research opportunities

## Interview Best Practices

### Before the Call
- Share template so client can prepare
- Set clear expectation: "This is detailed discovery, not sales"
- Block 60-90 minutes uninterrupted
- Ensure decision-maker is present

### During the Call
- Record (with permission) for reference
- Take notes in CLIENT'S EXACT LANGUAGE
- When they answer vaguely, dig deeper: "Can you give me a specific example?"
- Don't assume answers — ask, don't tell
- If they don't know a metric, note it as a gap to investigate

### After the Call
- Transcribe notes into Briefing Document within 24h
- Organize answers by category
- Flag gaps and assumptions for Phase 3 Research
- Prepare document for client review before moving to Phase 4

## Common Briefing Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Accepting vague answers** - Always ask for specifics and examples
2. **Not documenting exact wording** - This is your copy research
3. **Skipping metric questions** - Every baseline number matters for success measurement
4. **Not exploring pain deeply** - Ask "why" at least 3 times
5. **Failing to qualify constraints** - Legal/brand limits affect strategy options
