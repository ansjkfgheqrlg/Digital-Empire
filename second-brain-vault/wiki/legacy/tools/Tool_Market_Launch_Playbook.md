## Overview

The Market Launch Playbook Generator is a specialized tool for creating comprehensive product or service launch plans, including pre-launch, launch day, and post-launch strategies with specific tactics and timelines.

## Purpose

This tool generates launch strategies by:
- Creating complete launch timelines (4-12 weeks)
- Identifying target audiences and messaging
- Planning pre-launch awareness building
- Designing launch day activation strategy
- Creating post-launch nurture sequences
- Setting success metrics and monitoring plan

## Key Components

### Pre-Launch Phase (4-8 weeks before)
- Audience research and segmentation
- Value proposition and messaging development
- Content creation (guides, videos, case studies)
- Email list building
- Landing page creation
- Influencer/partner outreach

### Launch Week
- Press release distribution
- Email announcement sequences
- Social media blitz
- Paid advertising activation
- Community engagement
- Team coordination

### Post-Launch (Weeks 1-4)
- User onboarding sequences
- Early customer success focus
- Feedback collection
- Media and influencer follow-up
- Referral/affiliate activation
- Ongoing content marketing

## How It Applies to Digital Empire

The Launch Playbook applies to:
- **Service Line Launches**: New service offerings to market
- **Campaign Launches**: New lead generation campaigns
- **Client Projects**: Launching client websites or product updates
- **Product Launches**: New tool or offering releases

## Output Format

Generates:
- Complete launch timeline with specific dates
- Daily/weekly tactical checklist
- Messaging and positioning framework
- Content calendar for pre-launch
- Email sequence templates
- Social media content ideas
- Paid advertising strategy and budgets
- Success metrics and monitoring plan
