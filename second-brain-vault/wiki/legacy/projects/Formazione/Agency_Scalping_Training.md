## Project Overview

Agency Scalping is a comprehensive training program teaching entrepreneurs how to build and scale marketing agencies from zero. The program combines video content, case studies, and tactical frameworks for launching a profitable SMMA (Social Media Marketing Agency).

**Format**: Video series, PDFs, Case Studies
**Language**: Italian
**Status**: Content in Development
**Target Audience**: Aspiring agency owners, freelancers, entrepreneurs
**Content Modules**: 14+ PDF guides + video series

## Problem Solved

**"I want to start a marketing agency but don't know the systems, pricing, or sales process"**

This training addresses:
1. Lack of proven agency playbooks
2. Confusion about SMMA positioning and pricing
3. Fear of client acquisition at scale
4. Understanding agency economics and margins
5. Building repeatable systems

## Learning Objectives

After completing Agency Scalping training, students will:

- ✅ Understand the €14 million SMMA business model
- ✅ Create a go-to-market strategy for their agency
- ✅ Build repeatable service packages and pricing
- ✅ Master client outreach and sales systems
- ✅ Implement automation frameworks
- ✅ Scale from 1-person to multi-person agency
- ✅ Create recurring revenue models
